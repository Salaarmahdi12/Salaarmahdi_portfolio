INTER-UNIVERSITY SHOWCASE · 2025

University of Sindh
Software Project Expo

2nd Position · Team Achievement

Our team represented our institution at the Software Project Expo 2025, hosted at the University of Sindh, Naushahro Feroze Campus. Students from multiple universities came together to showcase their software projects and exchange ideas.

We presented our full-stack University Management System and earned 2nd position. The evaluation considered innovation, UI/UX, functionality and real-world applicability, with feedback from IT professionals, faculty and industry experts.

It was a proud team milestone and a valuable opportunity to demonstrate our work, learn from other participants and build confidence in presenting practical software solutions.
